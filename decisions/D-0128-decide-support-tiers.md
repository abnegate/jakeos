## Context
Public vocabulary for tested, community-reported and unsupported hardware with promotion rules must exist for the known-good list and V3 installer verdict (§62).

## Options

### Option A · Two tiers
Summary: Hardware is either supported or unsupported.
Consequences: Simple to explain; no room for community reports.
Evidence: none

### Option B · Three tiers
Summary: Lab-tested, community-reported and unsupported, with promotion rules into Tier 1.
Consequences: Nuanced guidance; a community tier to moderate.
Evidence: none

### Option C · Lab-only until 1.0
Summary: Only lab-tested hardware is listed until 1.0.
Consequences: Every listing is verified; a very short list.
Evidence: none
