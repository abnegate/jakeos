## Context
A certified machine is the strongest adoption vector and a scarce-engineering commitment, so OEM partnerships or a certification programme before 1.0 must be decided (§62, §70), answering Q-055.

## Options

### Option A · OEM partnerships before 1.0
Summary: The project pursues at least one OEM shipping or certifying a machine before 1.0.
Consequences: A real hardware channel at launch; engineering time is diverted to partner requirements.
Evidence: none

### Option B · Project-run certification programme before 1.0
Summary: The project publishes certification criteria and tests submitted machines.
Consequences: Community and vendors can self-certify; a programme to staff and a test suite to maintain.
Evidence: none

### Option C · Park both to post-1.0
Summary: Neither partnerships nor certification before 1.0.
Consequences: Focus on the stability contract; adoption relies on the Hardware Compatibility List alone.
Evidence: none

## Decision
Proposed. Not yet accepted.
