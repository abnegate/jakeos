## Context
A legal entity must hold trademarks, signing keys, shim, OIN and vendor agreements before V1 trademark filing and V3 public distribution (§56.4, §63); GOV-039 supplies jurisdiction evidence.

## Options

### Option A · Foundation
Summary: An independent non-profit foundation owns marks, keys and agreements.
Consequences: Neutral and durable ownership; incorporation cost and ongoing governance obligations.
Evidence: none

### Option B · Fiscal sponsor
Summary: An existing fiscal sponsor holds assets and signs on the project's behalf.
Consequences: Fast to set up with accounting handled; the project does not own its marks or keys outright.
Evidence: none

### Option C · Company
Summary: A company owns the assets.
Consequences: Simplest to form and operate; neutrality questions from contributors and vendors.
Evidence: none
