## Context
Who may extend Document, Mail, Calendar and Project and how RFCs land must be settled before the V2 automation-then-AI demo (§42, §44, §45, §57, §67).

## Options

### Option A · GOV-held standard catalogue extended only by RFC
Summary: The standard catalogue changes only through an RFC accepted by GOV.
Consequences: Stable vocabulary for automation and AI; slow growth and a bottleneck on GOV.
Evidence: none

### Option B · Open catalogue any publisher may extend
Summary: Any publisher may add Interfaces under its namespace.
Consequences: Rapid growth; fragmentation and duplicated verbs across publishers.
Evidence: none

### Option C · Hybrid with a frozen core plus optional extensions
Summary: A frozen core catalogue with namespaced publisher extensions that may be promoted by RFC.
Consequences: Stability where it matters and room to grow; two tiers to govern and document.
Evidence: none
