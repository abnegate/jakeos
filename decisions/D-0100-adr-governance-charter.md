## Context
External contributors will not invest without knowing how Decisions are made, so the governance charter naming appointment, removal, voting and conflict resolution must be adopted before the repository opens (§67, §70).

## Options

### Option A · BDFL
Summary: A single project lead makes final decisions with advisors.
Consequences: Fast, coherent direction; succession and single-point-of-failure risk.
Evidence: none

### Option B · Maintainer council
Summary: An elected council of maintainers votes on Decisions and appointments.
Consequences: Shared authority and legitimacy; slower consensus and election machinery to run.
Evidence: none

### Option C · Foundation technical steering committee
Summary: A foundation-hosted TSC governs under the foundation's bylaws.
Consequences: Institutional neutrality attractive to companies; formality and overhead before the project has a foundation.
Evidence: none
