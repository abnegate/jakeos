## Context
Hardware, CI, CDN and signing cost money before V2 lab and desktop Gates, so the funding model must be recorded with transparent finances (§56.4, §62), answering Q-053.

## Options

### Option A · Donations
Summary: Individual donations fund infrastructure with published accounts.
Consequences: Independence from any sponsor; income is unpredictable and may not cover a lab and CDN.
Evidence: none

### Option B · Sponsorship tiers
Summary: Corporate sponsors fund the project at published tiers.
Consequences: Predictable income for lab and CI; sponsors expect influence and the tier list must be governed.
Evidence: none

### Option C · Fiscal sponsor
Summary: A fiscal sponsor holds and disburses funds.
Consequences: Legal and accounting handled by others; fees and dependence on the sponsor's rules.
Evidence: none
