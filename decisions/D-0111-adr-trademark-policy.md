## Context
V3 exit requires a published trademark policy covering derivatives, spins, merchandise, compatible-with claims and unofficial builds (§63).

## Options

### Option A · Debian-style mark policy
Summary: Liberal use of the marks with clear rules for derivatives.
Consequences: Community-friendly and low enforcement burden; weaker control over confusing uses.
Evidence: none

### Option B · Fedora-style policy
Summary: Official marks only for official builds, with a remix mark for derivatives.
Consequences: Users can tell official from unofficial builds; a second mark to manage.
Evidence: none

### Option C · Rust-style policy
Summary: Strict mark use with explicit written permissions.
Consequences: Strong brand control; friction for community builds and merchandise.
Evidence: none
